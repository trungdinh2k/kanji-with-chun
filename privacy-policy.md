# Privacy Policy for Kanji with Chun

**Last updated: October 6, 2026**

Kanji with Chun ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

## 1. Personal Information

Kanji with Chun does not require users to create an account.

The App does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or address.

## 2. Learning Progress

Your learning progress and related preferences are stored locally on your device.

This information is used to provide features such as tracking learned kanji and maintaining your learning progress.

Kanji with Chun does not transmit this learning progress to the developer's servers.

## 3. Purchases

Kanji with Chun may offer in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Purchases are processed through Google Play. The App does not directly collect or store your payment card or banking information.

Google may process information related to purchases according to its own privacy policies.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App uses services provided by Google Play for app distribution and in-app purchase processing.

These services may collect or process certain information according to their own privacy policies.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Kanji with Chun does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 6. Data Security

Because learning progress is stored locally on your device, locally stored data may be lost if the App is uninstalled, app data is cleared, or the device is lost or reset.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time, for example when new features or services are added to the App.

Any changes will be reflected on this page by updating the "Last updated" date.
